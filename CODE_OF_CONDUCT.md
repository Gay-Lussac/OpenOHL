# Code of Conduct

OpenOHL adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct.

In short: be respectful and constructive, welcome people of every background and level of experience, critique ideas and calculations rather than people, and assume good faith. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not tolerated.

Engineering disagreements are settled with references, validation cases and reproducible results.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the project maintainers (currently [@Gay-Lussac](https://github.com/Gay-Lussac)). Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
