# Wood poles by SIA 265:2012

Implemented in `crates/ohl-structures-fe/src/sia265.rs` (`class`,
`eta_w`, `kc`, `lambda_rel`, `round_usage`), used by the pole check
(`crates/ohl-structures-fe/src/pole.rs`: `Pole::by_timber_code`,
`check_pole_factored`). Built, in active development (not approved yet);
pre-alpha: results must be verified by a qualified engineer.

**Source.** SIA 265:2012, *Construction en bois* (Swiss Society of
Engineers and Architects). Clause, table and formula numbers below are
the standard's.

## Where it is used

A pole (`.ohlp`, or `[pole]` in a structure file) of material `wood`,
solid (no `wall`), with its `strength_class` (C16, C24, C30 or D30) and
**no capacity given** (no `[[capacity]]` table, `moment_capacity` or
`fibre_stress`): checked by this page at the pole check's heights with
the forces of the [ASCE/SEI 48 page](asce48.md#forces-at-a-height) (the
moment with its P-Delta amplification when the pole gives its modulus).
`moisture_class` (1, 2 or 3) sets η_w; without it class 3
(**conservative**: a pole stands outdoors; said in what governs).

## Strengths

Round timber is treated as solid timber (3.3.1). The design values of
Table 6 (they hold the resistance factor: γ_M/η_M = 1.7 for round and
solid timber, Table 1), for the class:

| | C16 | C24 | C30 | D30 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| \\( f_{m,d} \\) (MPa) | 9.5 | 14 | 17.5 | 17 |
| \\( f_{c,0,d} \\) | 9.5 | 12 | 13.5 | 13 |
| \\( f_{v,d} \\) | 1.5 | 1.5 | 1.5 | 2.0 |
| \\( f_{c,0,k} \\) (Table 43) | 17 | 21 | 23 | 23 |
| \\( E_{0,05} \\) (Table 43) | 5300 | 7300 | 8000 | 8300 |

times η_w of the moisture class (Table 4: 1, 0.80, 0.60) and η_t = 1
(2.2.6: 1.4 only for shock-like actions; none taken). A pole holds its
pith: the shear values are halved (Table 6, note 6).

## Buckling

4.2.8: \\( k_c = 1/(k + \sqrt{k^2 - \lambda_{rel}^2}) \le 1 \\), \\( k =
0.5[1 + \beta_c(\lambda_{rel} - 0.3) + \lambda_{rel}^2] \\) (30, 31),
\\( \beta_c = 0.2 \\) (solid timber), \\( \lambda_{rel} =
\frac{\lambda}{\pi}\sqrt{f_{c,0,k}/E_{0,05}} \\) (34), at least 0.3.

![A tapered pole fixed at the ground line and free at its top, height H, its buckled shape, and the diameter at mid-height](../images/theory/pole-buckling.svg)

The pole as a cantilever fixed at the ground line: \\( L_K = 2H \\),
\\( \lambda = L_K/i \\) with \\( i = D/4 \\) of the diameter at mid-height.

**Simplification:** the mid-height radius for a tapered pole (SIA 265
gives no rule for tapered members): approximate, either way.

## The check

At each height, with \\( \sigma_{c,0,d} = N/A \\), \\( \sigma_{m,d} = M/W \\)
(\\( W = \pi D^3/32 \\)):

- in compression (37): \\( \dfrac{\sigma_{c,0,d}}{k_c f_{c,0,d}} +
  \dfrac{\sigma_{m,d}}{k_m f_{m,d}} \le 1 \\), \\( k_m = 1 \\) (a round
  section does not tip, 4.2.9);
- in tension: \\( \sigma_{m,d}/f_{m,d} \\);
- shear and torsion: \\( \tau = \dfrac{4V}{3A} + \dfrac{16T}{\pi D^3} \le
  f_{v,d}/2 \\) (**conservative**: the two largest added at one point).

The usage, times the load case's strength factor on the resistances, is
the largest; what governs names it with the class and η_w.

**Conservative:** the moments carry the P-Delta amplification and (37)
the buckling factor \\( k_c \\): the second order is counted twice.

## Not covered

Unsafe where one of them governs: the foundation (embedment), the
connections (bolts and crossarm fixings, SIA 265 chapter 6), holes and
notches in the shaft (5.2), decay and its protection (chapter 7),
deflection limits, fatigue (4.4).

## Validation

Unit tests (`sia265::tests`, `pole_extension_tests::wood_by_sia265_and_steel_by_sia263`):
Tables 6 and 43 as printed, \\( k_c \\) by hand, (35) against (34), a
section by (37) by hand, a pole at its ground line by hand, an unknown
class refused.
